# Code of conduct

This project follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).

Be respectful and constructive, assume good faith, welcome people of every background and level of experience, and keep discussions about the work. Harassment, insults, personal or political attacks, and publishing others' private information are not accepted, in issues, pull requests, discussions or any other project space.

## Reporting

If you witness or experience unacceptable behaviour, contact the maintainer privately through the contact links on his GitHub profile, [github.com/MaximeRumpler](https://github.com/MaximeRumpler). Reports are handled confidentially. The maintainer may remove comments or contributions, or block participants, who do not follow this code, as described in the enforcement guidelines of the Contributor Covenant.
