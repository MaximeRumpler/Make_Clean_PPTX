**Home energy: what actually moves the bill**

Heating and hot water make up about two thirds of a typical household's energy use.
Insulating the loft usually pays back in 3 to 5 years; replacing windows takes far longer.
Heat pumps cut heating energy by roughly a factor of three compared with resistance heating, but they need good insulation to shine.
Solar panels help most when you use power during the day; a battery is optional and rarely pays back on its own.
Quick wins: lower the thermostat by 1 °C, fix draughts, switch off standby loads.

Proposed plan: audit in month 1, insulation in months 2-3, heat pump in month 6, review after the first winter.
